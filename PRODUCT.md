# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vibe coders and indie builders — people shipping products fast, often with AI assistance. They arrive in two modes: **as makers**, wanting their work seen and upvoted (ego/visibility, "look what I built"), and **as browsers**, scanning what peers are shipping for signal and inspiration. They're technical, design-literate, and allergic to marketing fluff. Sign-in is Google/GitHub-only, so everyone here is a builder, not a passerby.

## Product Purpose

A showcase where builders post what they're building (name, screenshot, one-line tagline) and the community upvotes. Two feeds — **NEW** (freshest) and **HOT** (time-decayed vote score) — keep it moving. It exists to give vibe coders a lightweight, taste-forward place to be seen: lighter and less ceremonial than Product Hunt, more durable and focused than a Twitter post. Success = builders submit, share their product's page to pull their own audience in, and the feed stays fresh and credible. Each submission gets a shareable magazine-style OG card that does the outbound marketing.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, opinionated, builder-native. The voice is confident and concise with an actual point of view — peer-to-peer, written by someone who ships, for people who ship. No corporate hedging, no hype-deck superlatives, no hand-holding. The feeling to evoke: *"this site has taste"* — being featured here should feel like a small badge of credibility. Black-and-white magazine restraint over decorative chrome.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI-SaaS slop** — purple/indigo gradients, glassy cards, gradient text, identical icon-card grids, tiny tracked-uppercase eyebrows above every section. The "AI made this" look.
- **Corporate dashboard** — cold enterprise-admin chrome: sidebar + data tables + flat gray neutrals.
- **Crypto/Web3 maximalism** — neon-on-black, glows everywhere, aggressive animation, hype-coded surfaces.

## Design Principles

- **Aesthetics are the first filter.** The site's taste is what earns a builder's submission. If it doesn't look like it has a point of view, the people we want won't post. (审美即营销.)
- **Restraint over chrome.** Editorial calm. The products are the content; the UI gets out of the way. No card-grid reflex, no decorative effects that don't earn their place.
- **Built for people who ship.** Peer voice, terse copy, zero marketing fluff. Assume competence.
- **Pure signal.** OAuth-gated community keeps the feed credible; ranking rewards freshness and real votes and resists gaming (vote integrity is enforced server-side, not just visually).
- **Frictionless to be seen.** Posting and sharing should feel effortless; the auto-generated share card carries the outbound marketing so the builder doesn't have to.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (the ink/paper palette already favors this — keep muted grays honest, never light-gray-for-elegance). Full keyboard navigation with visible focus states. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: every animation needs a crossfade/instant fallback. Primary actions (vote, submit) carry accessible labels.
